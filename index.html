Sikaly

Simple money tracking for traders, individuals and small businesses.

Record your sales and expenses, see who owes you, and understand your profit. Sikaly works offline and keeps your records on your phone.

Android app. Coming soon to Google Play.

What it does

  • Sales and expensesAdd entries in seconds, with categories made for everyday trading.
  • Who owes meTrack customers on credit and record part-payments.
  • Daily profitSee today's money in, money out and profit at a glance.
  • ChartsWeekly, monthly and yearly views, with a spending breakdown.
  • Three modesTrader, Personal or Company, each with the right categories.
  • Private by designNo account, no ads, and the app does not connect to the internet.
  • PIN lockKeep your records safe if someone borrows your phone.
  • Backup and exportSave a backup file, or export a spreadsheet for your accountant.
