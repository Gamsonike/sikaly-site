Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Sikaly is a money-tracking app for traders, individuals and small businesses. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your records stay on your phone, and we do not collect them.

Who we are

Sikaly is published by [Isaac Gamah], Ghana. You can reach us at isaacgamah.dev@gmail.com.

What the app stores

When you use Sikaly, you may enter income and expense records (amounts, categories, notes and dates), the names and amounts of people who owe you money, and your settings (currency and mode). All of this is saved in a database inside the app on your device.

What we collect

We collect nothing. Sikaly has no accounts or sign-in. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and it is built without permission to connect to the internet. Your records are never sent to us or to any server we run, and we cannot see them.

When data leaves your phone

  • When you export. If you tap the export button, Sikaly creates a CSV file and opens your phone’s share menu. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as WhatsApp, email or Google Drive. After that, the app you chose handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Android backup. Depending on your phone’s settings, Android may back up app data to your Google account. That is a feature of your device and Google, not something Sikaly controls, and Google’s terms apply to it.

Information about other people

If you record someone’s name in the “who owes me” or receivables section, that information stays on your device and is your responsibility. Keep it accurate, use it only for your business or personal records, and follow the data protection laws that apply to you.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete individual entries inside the app by swiping them away. To remove everything, uninstall Sikaly or clear its storage in your phone’s Settings under Apps. Because we hold no copy, deleting it from your phone deletes it completely, unless you exported a file or have a device backup. In that case, delete those copies as well.

Children

Sikaly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and similar laws elsewhere, people have rights over their personal data. Because we do not collect or hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on our side. You are in control of everything stored on your device. If you have questions, write to us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sikaly handles information, for example by adding cloud backup, we will update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect, and we will tell you in the app where the change matters.
